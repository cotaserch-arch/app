# Botfolio: business plan

## The honest part first

No app comes with a guaranteed billion-dollar valuation or $1M in year one. Most consumer apps never take off, and the ones that do usually need many tries. This plan gives Botfolio its best shot: a new kind of product in a space people are obsessed with (AI and crypto), built so every user brings in more users, with several ways to make money from day one.

## The idea

**Fantasy sports for AI trading bots.**

Anyone can describe a trading strategy in one sentence. AI turns it into a bot, tests it, grades it, and enters it in a live league where it trades paper money against everyone else's bots. You watch your bot climb the leaderboard, earn badges, battle rivals and get hyped by the community.

- **Nobody can lose money.** It's a game, which keeps it fun, legal and easy to share.
- **It's genuinely new.** In late 2025, a public experiment where a handful of big AI models traded crypto against each other (Nof1's "Alpha Arena") drew huge attention. People love watching AIs compete for money. Botfolio opens that up so *anyone* can build their own AI trader and compete.
- **It rides three huge waves at once:** AI agents, crypto, and the "everyone's an investor" generation.

## Why people will come back every day

| Hook | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Your bot is alive** | It trades every day without you. Checking on it is a daily habit, like checking a fantasy team. |
| **Leaderboard & ranks** | Status and competition. "My bot is #12 in the world." |
| **Bot Battles** | Instant rivalry between friends and strangers. Every battle is a shareable link. |
| **AI Coach grades** | "My bot got an A" is a brag; "mine got an F" is a challenge. Both get shared. |
| **Achievements** | Collectible badges like Bitcoin Beater, Sniper and Whale give people goals. |
| **Clone & improve** | Copying the leader and making a "2.0" keeps creators competing. |
| **Hype** | Social proof, and a Trending board that rewards personality, not just returns. |

**The growth loop:** build a bot → share its rank or a battle → a friend clicks and builds a bot to beat it → they share theirs.

## How it makes money

| Stream | How | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Ads** | Finance and crypto are among the highest-paying ad categories. Ad slots are already built in (AdSense or direct). | At launch |
| **Season sponsors** | Exchanges and crypto apps already pay for trading competitions to reach traders. Sell "Season 2 presented by ___", with sponsor-funded prizes. | Once traffic is steady |
| **Pro ($9/mo) and Whale ($29/mo)** | More bots, instant trade alerts, deep AI reviews, private leagues, no ads. The waitlist is live. | Month 2–4 |
| **Strategy marketplace** | Top creators sell their bots' rules; Botfolio keeps 20–30%. | Month 6+ |
| **Creator leagues** | Influencers run branded leagues for their followers (paid placement or revenue share). | Month 6+ |
| **Affiliate partnerships** | Referral deals with exchanges and crypto apps. Use with care and clear disclosure (see Risks). | Later |

## One way to reach $1M a year

$1M a year is about **$83,000 a month**. One possible mix, with every number an assumption to test, not a promise:

| Source | Assumption | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Ads | 150,000 monthly users × 20 page views × $10 per 1,000 views | $30,000 |
| Pro & Whale | 2% of users subscribe at an average of $12 | $36,000 |
| Season sponsors | One $50,000 sponsor per quarter | $16,700 |
| **Total** | | **≈ $83,000** |

So the real goal is **about 150,000 monthly active users** who love the game. Everything below is about getting there.

## Launch plan

1. **Ship it** (this week): deploy, add the AI key, buy a domain.
2. **Seed the league:** create 20–30 bots yourself with fun personalities. Invite 50 friends and crypto-curious people to build theirs.
3. **Content engine:** post short videos every week: *"I let 100 AI bots trade crypto for a week. Here's who won."* *"My AI bot vs. Bitcoin, 30 days."* *"Roasting your trading bots."* The leaderboard writes the content for you.
4. **Creators:** give crypto and finance creators their own league and a battle against their audience.
5. **Seasons:** run monthly seasons with a reset, a champion and badges. Bring in a sponsor by Season 2 or 3.
6. **Community:** a Discord where people argue about strategies, with weekly battle tournaments.
7. **App stores:** the web app is installable now. Then Google Play (fast) and the Apple App Store (needs native features; see README).

## Numbers to watch every week

1. **Build rate:** % of visitors who build a bot (target 25%+)
2. **Enter rate:** % of built bots entered into the league
3. **Day-7 return:** % of bot owners who come back within a week (target 30%+)
4. **Share rate and invites per user** (the growth loop)
5. **Waitlist signups**, the clearest signal of who will pay

## Risks, honestly

| Risk | What to do about it |
|---|---|
| **Regulation** | Keep it paper-only, with no deposits, no custody and no personalized advice. **Don't launch a token**: it brings securities risk and attracts scams. Prize contests need official rules (talk to a lawyer before cash prizes). |
| **"Is this financial advice?"** | Disclaimers are already on every page. The AI coach is told never to recommend real trades. |
| **Being a fad** | Seasons, new coins, stocks, private leagues and creator leagues keep it fresh. |
| **Copycats** | Move fast. Community, creator relationships and bot history are hard to copy. |
| **AI costs** | Cents per bot, plus rate limits per visitor. Pro covers heavy users. |
| **App store rejection** | Launch on the web first, and add native features before submitting to Apple. |

## What only you can do (the next steps)

2. **Create a Render or Railway account** and deploy (steps in the README, about 10 minutes).
3. **Buy a domain.** On Oct 14, 2026, `botfolio.com` and `botfolio.ai` were taken and `botfolioleague.com` was available. Availability changes, so register promptly at any registrar. Because the short domains are taken, **search the "Botfolio" trademark before investing in the brand**. If someone already uses it, rename now (the name appears in only a handful of files).
4. **Form a company** (an LLC is fine) before taking money.
5. **Apply for Google AdSense** once the site is live.
6. **Developer accounts:** Google Play ($25 once) and Apple ($99/year). Both require your personal identity.
7. **Post the first "AI bots battle" video** and invite your first 50 players.
