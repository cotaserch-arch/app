# Clutch: business plan

## The honest part first

No app comes with a guaranteed fortune. Most consumer apps never take off. This plan gives Clutch its best shot:

- **A huge, obsessive audience:** sports fans check scores and argue about games every day.
- **A fresh hook:** you vs. the AI.
- **Several revenue lines** that sports already proves out.

The goal of the first months isn't money. It's people coming back every day.

## The idea

**The AI sports app: four ways to play, one profile.**

1. **Beat the Bot:** pick winners against an AI analyst that shows its picks and reasoning.
2. **Prediction market:** trade YES/NO on outcomes with free coins.
3. **Betting-bot league:** describe a strategy, and AI builds a bot that competes.
4. **Watch parties:** live rooms with AI commentary and fan chat.

Each one alone is a known category. Putting them together around an AI rival is new, and gives a fan four reasons to open the app on every game day.

## Why people come back daily

| Hook | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **You vs. the Bot scoreboard** | A clear rival with a public record. "I'm 23-19 against the AI" is a brag. |
| **Daily coins and streaks** | Habit loop. The bonus grows with consecutive days. |
| **Live games** | Watch parties turn every game into a social event inside the app. |
| **Markets that move** | Prices change as fans trade. Checking your positions is addictive. |
| **Your bot works while you sleep** | Bots bet every matching game automatically. |
| **Push alerts** | "✅ Your pick won! You beat the Bot." brings people back at the right moment. |
| **Referrals** | 500 coins for both sides. Every share is an invite. |

## How it makes money

| Stream | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sportsbook affiliate deals** | Legal sportsbooks pay for each new customer you refer. These are among the most valuable affiliate payouts online. | Website only, 21+, legal states, licensed where required. Never inside the phone app. |
| **Ads** | Sports traffic is high-volume and advertisers love it. AdSense or direct deals (sports drinks, streaming, gear). | Ad slots are already built in. |
| **Sponsored seasons and contests** | "Weekly Pick'em presented by ___", with sponsor-funded prizes. | Prize contests need official rules and free entry. Talk to a lawyer first. |
| **Clutch Pro ($4.99/mo)** | Full AI breakdowns, AI commentary everywhere, 10 bots, private leagues, no ads. | Web: Stripe. Phone app: Apple/Google in-app purchase (required). |
| **Leagues ($49/season)** | Branded leagues for offices, bars and fan clubs. | Great for B2B word of mouth. |
| **Creator leagues** | Sports creators run leagues and watch parties for their audience, on a revenue share. | Creators bring their own audience. |

## One way to reach $1M a year

$1M a year is about **$83,000 a month**. One possible mix. Every number is an assumption to test, not a promise:

| Source | Assumption | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Sportsbook referrals | 300 new depositing customers a month × $150 average payout | $45,000 |
| Ads | 100,000 monthly fans × 25 page views × $8 per 1,000 views | $20,000 |
| Pro | 3,000 subscribers × $4.99 | $15,000 |
| Sponsors and leagues | A couple of small deals a month | $5,000 |
| **Total** | | **≈ $85,000** |

The real target is roughly **100,000 monthly fans** who use the app weekly. That's the number to chase.

## Launch plan

1. **Ship it.** Deploy the site, add the AI key and a contact email, buy a domain.
2. **Pick a moment.** Launch into a big sports moment (NFL Sundays, NBA opening week, March Madness) and own one sport first.
3. **Seed the community.** Invite 100 friends, group chats and local sports bars. Run a "beat the Bot" contest with a sponsor-funded prize.
4. **Content machine.** Post weekly short videos: "The AI went 9-3 this week. Can you beat it?", "Fans vs. AI: who called the upset?" The leaderboards and the Bot's record write the content for you.
5. **Creators.** Give sports creators a branded league and watch party. They bring their audience.
6. **App stores.** Ship the phone app (see `app-store/README.md`) once the website has steady users.
7. **Turn on money in order:** ads, then sportsbook affiliate (website, after legal review), then Pro.

## Numbers to watch weekly

1. **Day-1 and day-7 return rates.** Target 40% and 20%.
2. **Picks per fan per week.** Engagement depth.
3. **Watch-party messages per live game.** Community health.
4. **Invites per fan.** The growth loop.
5. **Revenue per 1,000 fans.** Ads plus affiliate.

## Risks, honestly

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Gambling law** | Clutch stays free to play: no coin purchases, no cash value, no real-money prizes. Sportsbook affiliate deals need legal review, state licensing where required, 21+ only, and responsible-gambling messages (built in). |
| **App store rules** | Built in already: no sportsbook links or AdSense inside the app, account deletion, chat reporting and blocking, no non-Apple payments. Expect an 18+ age rating. |
| **Sports data** | The current feed is ESPN's unofficial one. Budget for a licensed provider before scaling. |
| **AI mistakes** | The Bot's record is public, so mistakes are part of the fun. Previews only use provided data and never state news as fact. |
| **Competition** (ESPN, Underdog, PrizePicks, Kalshi…) | Win on "you vs. AI" + community + free-to-play. Avoid fighting real-money apps head on. |
| **Moderation** | Profanity filter, reports, auto-hide and blocking are built in. Review reported messages in the admin stats weekly. |
