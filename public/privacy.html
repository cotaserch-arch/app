Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Summary: Clutch collects as little as possible. You play with a handle you choose. There's no email, password or phone number needed to play.

What we store

  • Your profile: your handle, coin balance, picks, market trades, bots and chat messages.
  • A fan code saved in your browser so the app remembers you. Anyone with this code can use your profile, so keep it private.
  • Your email, only if you join the Pro waitlist or send a partner inquiry, and only to contact you about that.
  • Basic server logs (like IP addresses) for security and rate limiting, kept for a short time.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't take real-money bets, and coins have no cash value.

AI features

Game previews, bot building and commentary may be generated by an AI provider (Anthropic). When you describe a betting bot, that text is sent to the provider to build your bot. Don't include personal information in it.

Ads and partners

Clutch may show ads (for example through Google AdSense), which can use cookies to measure and personalize ads. Partner links are clearly labeled. When you follow one, the partner's own privacy policy applies.

Chat

Watch-party messages are public to everyone in that game's room and are deleted after a few days.

Children

Clutch is not intended for children under 13. Partner offers are only for adults 21+ in legal jurisdictions.

Deleting your data

Email the address below with your handle and we'll delete your profile.
