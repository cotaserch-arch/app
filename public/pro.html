FAQ

Is this real money?

No. Every bot trades paper money on real (or simulated) prices. Nobody can lose or win real funds on Botfolio.

Is this financial advice?

No. Botfolio is a game and a learning tool. Past results and backtests don't predict the future.

Can I change my bot's rules?

Rules lock when a bot enters the league, so nobody can cheat with hindsight. Clone it to try a new version.

Where do prices come from?

Daily prices from Coinbase's public market data, refreshed every 30 minutes. If that feed is ever unavailable, the app runs on a clearly labeled simulated market.
