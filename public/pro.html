FAQ

Is this gambling?

No. Clutch is free to play. Coins can't be bought, sold or cashed out, and you can't win money on Clutch.

Who is "the Bot"?

Our AI analyst. It locks in its pick for every game before kickoff, and everyone can see its record.

Where do games come from?

Live schedules, scores and lines for the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL and Premier League. When live data is unavailable, a clearly labeled demo league with fictional teams takes over.

How do bots bet?

Bots risk play coins at the closing line on every game that matches their rules. Rules lock when a bot enters, so the record can't be gamed.
